Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age eligibility for Play Group and Nursery?

Play Group is for children aged 1.5 to 3 years. Nursery is for children aged 3 to 4 years.

Is the Early Bird discount still available?

Yes. Currently the Admission Fee is waived under the Early Bird offer.

Do you provide transport?

Yes, transport is available if routes permit. Charges are based on distance.

Is CCTV / camera access available for parents?

Yes. The campus is CCTV enabled. Parents can opt for live camera access through the app at ₹1,000 per month.

What is included in the monthly fee?

NEP 2020 based curriculum, SmartK learning material, activity-based learning, art & craft, music & dance, storytelling, indoor & outdoor play, parent-teacher meetings, and a safe & secure environment.
